Agil Temporal Journey

Player plays as Agil Kilometer and roams around Chrono Islet. Starts in the home town, Polestar Village. Roam around to different areas to find the Chrono Syndicate. Travel around to explore and meet new partners like Tanner Clawitzer, Loona Weston, Finnegan Deluge, and Wallace Drachonos. Fight against Karober Tyranno, the ancient dinosaur that plans to wipe out the world back to the Prehistoric Era. The Chrono Syndicate will provide shards of the Nebula Crystal to use against the Chrono Star, the main weapon against the world's end.
